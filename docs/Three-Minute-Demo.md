# AI60 - Three-minute video script and shot list

Target duration: 3:00. Record the deployed app in simulation mode. Use fictional contact details. This is a script, not a claim that a narrated submission video has been recorded.

## 0:00-0:25 - Show the landing page

"The challenge is to get 500 final-year engineering students to register for a free AI workshop in seven days with 2,000 rupees. I targeted students who know some programming but do not yet have an AI project they can explain. My hypothesis is that a concrete build outcome is a stronger reason to register than a generic workshop promise."

## 0:25-0:55 - Open the SQL example and project matcher

"The page shows exactly what a student could build. This is a prepared input and checked example output, clearly labeled. The workshop scope is a guided prototype, with limitations stated. Students can register immediately or answer three optional questions to find a suitable project. There is no invented résumé score."

## 0:55-1:20 - Register and reveal the pass

"Registration comes before teamwork. A successful response produces a workshop pass with a preparation checklist and starter kit. Simulation passes are visibly labeled, so no real event enrollment is implied. The date is still unconfirmed. If a configured database fails, the application reports an error rather than inventing success."

## 1:20-1:45 - Create a squad and preview the invitation

"Squads are optional. Builder, Solver, and Shipper have practical tasks: connect, test, and explain. The invitation is about the specific project, and it leads directly to registration. Filling a squad is never a condition for attending. Share actions are measured separately from delivered invitations."

## 1:45-2:25 - Show Command Center and change campus conversion

"The baseline assumes 20 campus contacts producing 50 unique visits each at 30 percent conversion: 300 registrations. Relevant communities contribute 125 and a single referral generation adds about 75. These are assumptions, not campaign results. Lower campus conversion to 20 percent and the forecast falls to 382. The model exposes the gap and keeps 500 rupees for a measurable recovery action. Audience overlap is editable, and no viral compounding is assumed."

## 2:25-2:45 - Show plan and learning notes

"I prioritized campus partners, relevant communities, and optional peer invitations. The seven-day plan starts with a pilot before expanding. I rejected compulsory referrals, artificial countdowns, and unvalidated readiness scoring. I also corrected inconsistent seeded metrics and removed silent success after backend failures."

## 2:45-3:00 - End on evaluator page

"With another day I would observe five student usability sessions, validate the channel assumptions, and verify database concurrency on staging. This submission shows the working asset, the growth economics, and where the plan could fail. The goal is not to claim 500 registrations happened. It is to show a credible way to pursue them."

## Recording checklist

- Use the current branch and production server, not an old dev session.
- Keep SIMULATION labels visible.
- Show one prepared output, one registration, one invitation, and one changed assumption.
- Do not reveal contact details, secrets, or database credentials.
- Verify the uploaded recording is approximately three minutes and understandable without source code.
